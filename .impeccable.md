# Impeccable — Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

Fellow HCI/AI researchers, academic recruiters, hiring managers, prospective
collaborators, and students. They arrive to evaluate Bruce Liu's work — often
skimming on desktop or phone, looking to understand who he is, read his papers,
grab his CV, and get a sense of the person behind the research. This audience
_notices_ craft; small details are read as signals of care.

### Brand Personality

Precise, understated, quietly curious. A researcher's portfolio — intellectually
playful in copy ("agents that develop their own taste") but visually restrained.
Three words: **precise, understated, curious**. It should feel like the work of
someone who sweats the details without announcing that they do.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined minimalism. Monochrome neutrals (OKLCH, near-zero chroma) with a single
warm accent — gold/amber — reserved strictly for genuine achievement (award
badges), and coordinating with the amber sun icon in the theme switch.
Geist for text, Geist Mono available. Generous whitespace, left-aligned, narrow
reading column (max-w-screen-sm). Motion-forward but disciplined: blur-in section
reveals, magnetic links, a tilting profile photo, a text-scramble name. Both
light and dark themes (system default). Anti-references: flashy startup landing
pages, neon gradients, glassmorphism, gimmicky microcopy.

### Design Principles

1. **Restraint over spectacle.** Every effect must survive the 100th viewing.
   If a visitor notices the delight more than the content, it's gone too far.
2. **Motion conveys state, never decorates.** Entrances, feedback, and
   attention rewards — not ambient animation for its own sake.
3. **Reward attention.** The best details surface only on interaction (hover,
   cursor, tap), so the resting page stays calm and the curious get a payoff.
4. **Monochrome discipline, one accent.** Gold/amber appears only for awards.
   Neutrals stay neutral. Color scarcity is what gives the accent its meaning.
5. **Accessible by default.** Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`,
   keeps keyboard focus legible, and never hides content behind an effect.
